FAQ

Is this a physical binder?
No. This is a digital PDF download. Print the pages and add them to your own folder or binder.
Can I type into the PDFs?
This version is designed to print and complete by hand.
Do I need to use every page?
No. Start with the pages that solve your most immediate need.
Is this medical advice?
No. The kit helps organize information and record instructions from your care team. It does not replace professional advice.
Where should I keep completed pages?
Store them securely and share sensitive information only with the people who need it.
